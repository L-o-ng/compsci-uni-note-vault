#notes

The goal of **Named Entity Recognition** (*NER*) is to identify *named entities* from within *unstructured text*.
![[Named Entity Recognition.png]]
## Named Entity Disambiguation
*NER* gets the ==strings of tokens==, and ==types of entity==. **Named Entity Disambiguation** (*NED*) tells us which things these are, with some reference to some knowledge base (often Wikipedia, Wikidata, or Dbpedia).
