## Acronym
+ **S** - *Specific*: The RQ must be specifically stated;
+ **M** - *Measurable*: The RQ should be quantifiable or observable;
+ **A** - *Attainable*: The RQ should be easily achieved, solved, answered, or investigated by the researcher;
+ **R** - *Realistic*: It should be possible for the researcher to perform the study to investigate the problem;
+ **T** - *Timely*: The study should be carried out within the given time period. 
## Example
"Within the next 12 months, to what extent does daily usage of social media platforms (measured in hours) correlate with the severity of self-reported anxiety and depression symptoms (using the Generalized Anxiety Disorder 7-item scale and the Patient Health Questionnaire-9, respectively) in U.S. young adults aged 18-25?”
