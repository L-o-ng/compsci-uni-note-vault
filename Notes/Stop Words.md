#definition 

Some words may not appear to contribute much *content* to the text.
Suppose we asked ==what the key concepts in a text were==?
Here:
+ `and`, `or`, `is`, `of`,  `in`, `at`, `are`, `not`, etc are likely to be the **most frequent words**;
+ This does not tell us about *concepts*.

These words are called **stop words** - words we choose to remove entirely:
+ `This film was great => film great`;
+ `The film was not great => film great`.
