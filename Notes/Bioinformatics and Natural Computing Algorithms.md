**Phylogenetics** refers to the evolutionary relationships between species. The idea first arose in Darwin's Theory of *Evolution*, which discussed the idea of a phylogenetic tree.
The study of bioinformatics thus helps us:
+ Discover the **origin** of various *diseases*;
+ Quantify biodiversity, hence informing conservation policy.

Darwin based his tree on observed **characters**: the number of legs, type of coat, etc.
*Modern* phylogenetics uses **DNA** or protein sequence data. Our human genome has *3 billion* characters. We have a vast amount of data and so a significant computational issue.
