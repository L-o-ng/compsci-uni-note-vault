#notes 

There are 3 *characterisations* of data:
+ Structured;
+ Semi-structured;
+ Unstructured.
## Structured Data
This is data represented in a fixed format: a **schema**. It typically uses a [[Entity Relationship Modelling|Relational Data Model]]. The DBMS checks to ensure that the structures, integrity, and referential constraints are followed.
## Semi-Structured Data
This can also be referred to as **self-describing** or **schema-less** data.
This is data that may be *irregular* or *incomplete*, and may have a structure that changed *rapidly* and *unpredictably*.
There may be some structure, but not all parts have the *same* structure, and each object may have different attributes that are *not* known in advance.
## Unstructured Data
There is a very limited indication of the type/structure of the data. Typical examples are text/html documents containing some information.
