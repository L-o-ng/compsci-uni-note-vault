#notes 

**Particle** systems are used for simulating chaotic or fluid phenomena that are difficult to model with traditional geometry.
A system manages a large number of simple objects called particles. Each is an independent entity with attributes such as:
+ Position;
+ Velocity;
+ Colour;
+ Size;
+ Lifetime;
+ etc.

An **emitter** generates particles, and physics updates their state each frame. They are rendered as [[Billboards]].
