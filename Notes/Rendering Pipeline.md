The **rendering pipeline** is the sequence of steps a GPU follows to convert a 3D scene into a 2D image.
1. *Input*: Vertex data, textures, and camera settings;
2. *Process*: Data moves through distinct **stages**, some of which are programmable;
3. *Output*: A finished 2D image is stored in the **framebuffer**.
