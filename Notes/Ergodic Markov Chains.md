#notes 

A [[Finite Markov Chains|Finite Markov Chain]] that is irreducible and aperiodic is called **ergodic**. It has a unique stationary distribution $\pi(\pi M=\pi)$, and $uM^{n}\to \pi$ for every initial distribution $u$.

This is easily seen when representing the Markov chain as a digraph with weighted edges:
![[Ergodic Markov Chains.png]]
The Markov chain is a random walk in the digraph. The graph is **strongly connected** as you can reach any vertex from any vertex, and thus we call it **irreducible**.
![[Ergodic Markov Chains-1.png]]

See [[Graph Theory]] for more information on graphs.
