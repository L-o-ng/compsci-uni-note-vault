An image is a captured graphical representation of a **scene**, encoded into a 2D signal. We will focus on **assistive imaging**: the *enhancement*, *restoration*, *representation*, or *transformation* of visual data to aid in visualisation and interpretation by humans, or as a pre-processing step for computer vision.
