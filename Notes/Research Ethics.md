> Research ethics concerns the responsibility of researchers to be **honest** and **respectful** to all individuals who are affected by their research studies or their reports of the studies' results.
## Integrity vs Ethics
+ **Research integrity** means conducting research in a way which allows others to have *trust* and *confidence* in the methods used and the findings that result from this. Within the **University**, conducting research with integrity also means meeting the *professional standards* expected of our researchers.
+ **Research ethics** govern the *standards* of conduct for scientific researchers. It is important to adhere to ethical principles in order to protect the *dignity*, *rights* and *welfare* of research participants.
## Key Ethical Issues
1. Acknowledge sources;
2. Avoid bias;
3. Avoid conflict of interest;
4. Work with other researchers in a collegial manner;
5. Be safe;
6. Consider environmental and societal good;
7. Keep accurate records;
8. Be fiscally responsible;
9. Follow appropriate channels to complain;
10. Take proper care when involving people in research.
## Ethics Checklist
1. Have you prepared a briefing script for volunteers?
2. Will the participants be using non-standard hardware?
3. Is there any intentional deception of the participants?
4. How will participants voluntarily give consent?
5. Will the participants be exposed to any risks greater than those encountered in their normal working life?
6. Are you offering any incentive to the participants?
7. Are any participants under 16?
8. Do any participants have an impairment that will limit their understanding or communication?
9. Are you in a position of authority or influence over any participants?
10. Will the participants be informed of their right to withdraw?
11. Will the participants be informed of your contact details?
12. Will participants be debriefed?
13. Will the data collected be stored in an anonymous form?
## Professional Ethics
The requirement for ethically responsible research, innovation, and implementation of computing systems has resulted in various professional bodies creating guidelines:
+ [BCS Code of Conduct](https://www.bcs.org/membership/become-a-member/bcs-code-of-conduct/)
+ [ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct](https://www.acm.org/code-of-ethics#:~:text=Preamble,the%20conscience%20of%20the%20profession)
+ [IEEE Code of Ethics](https://www.ieee.org/about/corporate/governance/p7-8.html)
+ [IET Rules of Conduct](https://www.theiet.org/about/governance/rules-of-conduct/)
