#notes 

Every TM $\mathscr{M}$ can be **encoded** as a *word* over a finite alphabet. We shall use $\langle \mathscr{M}\rangle$ to denote the *encoding* of $\mathscr{M}$.
>There is a TM $\mathscr{U}$ that takes a 2-part input: the encoding of a TM $\mathscr{M}$, $\langle \mathscr{M}\rangle$ and a word $w$, and simulate $\mathscr{M}$ on $w$. $\mathscr{U}$ is then called a **Universal Turing Machine**.
