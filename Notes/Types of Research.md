## Types of Research
### Positivist
A **positivist** study is:
+ Objective;
+ *Reductionist*: breaking down complexities into simpler units for study;
+ Concerning verifying theories.

It can be studied in isolation.
### Critical Theorist
A **critical theorist** study is:
+ Concerning *empowering* people with knowledge;
+ Participatory;
+ Seeks to bring about change.

It focuses on empowering groups, and is studied within that context.
### Constructivist
A **constructivist** study is:
+ Where truth is *relative* to context;
+ Where theory is open to *interpretation*;
+ Concerning *generating* theories in a given context.

It **cannot** be studied in isolation.
### Pragmatist
A **pragmatist** study is:
+ Where all research is *biased*;
+ Where there is no objective truth;
+ Concerning working towards practical solutions to problems.

It focuses on finding the best answer(s) to a problem out of many.
